### Quick Buying Tips

1. **Check warranty** – Bentley watches typically carry a 2‑year manufacturer warranty; ensure the retailer lists it.
2. **Verify authenticity** – Look for the original serial number, warranty card, and a reputable seller (official Bentley boutique or authorized dealer).
3. **Consider shipping & taxes** – Prices shown online often exclude import duties; factor those in if buying internationally.
4. **Compare return policies** – Luxury watches are a bigger investment; a flexible return window is valuable.
